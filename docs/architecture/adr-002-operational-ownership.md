# ADR-002: Operational ownership

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Trama owns the operational health of active Plots, OpenLoops, and Actions. The
system is responsible for maintaining status hygiene, priority, priority
reasons, and next review dates.

The user retains authority over real-world facts, personal decisions, and
semantic conclusions. Deterministic derived fields may update automatically;
interpretive changes must be proposed for review.
